## シート01

Use case: precise-object-edit / production 2D character rig texture.
Edit this approved character sheet with the smallest possible changes. Preserve the exact character designs, silhouettes, poses, proportions, colour fills, soft cel shading, outline thickness, anatomical parts, spacing and relative sizes. Preserve the original horizontal arrangement and all character positions.
Required edits ONLY:
1. Remove the white paper/background completely, making the background truly transparent, including gaps between legs, horns, tails, tentacles, fins and ears.
2. Remove all Japanese text labels and any other lettering completely.
3. Remove all EYES and MOUTHS from every character, replacing only those tiny marks with a perfectly matching continuation of the existing face/body colour and shading. The face must be blank where eyes and mouth were. This is a blank puppet texture for animated facial overlays.
Keep NOSES, whiskers, cheeks/blush, crescent moon, antlers, shell crystal, mane flames and all other existing features unchanged. If a nose and mouth connect, keep the nose and remove the mouth below it.
Do not simplify the design further, do not redesign, do not add features, do not change the head direction, do not split into parts, do not duplicate or add characters, do not rearrange. No drop shadows, background tint, outline halo or fake checkerboard. Transparent background with clean antialiased edges.
Sheet contains four subjects: golden jellyfish, green leafy deer, pink sheep, orange fire lion. All must remain fully visible. The rendered artwork will be skinned to a mesh in Rive; preserving the design and placement is essential.
